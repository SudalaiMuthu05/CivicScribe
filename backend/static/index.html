How The Grievance Scribe Works

        

          Learn how your citizen request is transformed into a legally structured petition.
        

      


      

        
1. Grievance vs. RTI Request

        

          Public Grievances: Use this when public infrastructure is damaged, public services are failing, or government authorities have neglected a duty (e.g., potholes, broken street lights, contaminated water).
        

        

          Right to Information (RTI): Use this to inspect government records, seek copies of expenditure logs, tenders, or verify progress on official decisions under the RTI Act, 2005.
        

      


      

        
2. Fact Grounding & Guidance

        

          The AI only drafts using the concrete facts you provide. It references official government rules and structures without inventing details.
        

      


      

        
3. Submitting to Government Authorities

        

          This application formats and creates an official case draft on your device. To submit it, copy or print your finalized draft and present it to the designated department office, post it by registered post, or file it via your state's online portal (e.g. CPGRAMS or Online RTI).
